## 🎯 What Is This Project?

The **Wellness Marketplace for Alternative Therapies** is a **full-stack web application** that connects:

- 👤 **Patients** looking for alternative therapy services
- 🧑‍⚕ **Practitioners** offering certified wellness therapies
- 🛡 **Admins** who verify practitioners and manage the platform

The platform ensures **trust, security, and scalability** through role-based access and verification workflows.

---

## 🧩 Core Modules

### 👤 Patient
- Register & login
- Browse practitioners
- Search therapies
- Book appointments
- View booking history

### 🧑‍⚕ Practitioner
- Register as practitioner
- Upload verification details
- Manage profile & services
- Handle appointments
- Track verification status

### 🛡 Admin
- View unverified practitioners
- Review uploaded documents
- Approve or reject practitioners
- Assign specialization & rating
- Monitor system activity
